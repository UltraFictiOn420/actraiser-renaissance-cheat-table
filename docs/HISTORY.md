# Development history

1. Initial HP hooks also affected enemies or failed to protect the player. The player/enemy distinction was corrected, with v4 reported working through a level.
2. Angel/settlement support expanded to SP, resources, usable items, champion chronicles/stats, forts, and fences. The redundant Philotas-only 999 toggle was removed in favor of broader champion coverage.
3. Act MP and damage modifiers needed corrections. Act Death/Loot Aura experiments were abandoned after gameplay and stability problems.
4. No Player Hit Detection became the preferred Act invincibility option; v20 was reported working well.
5. v21 was retained and later reported working over many hours.
6. v22-v29 explored collectible teleportation, including several hotkey changes and target/movement approaches. After crashes and wall entrapment, the user requested reverting to the pre-teleport table.

Surviving versions: v1-v7 and v20-v29. v8-v19 were not found in the inspected output or retained Actraiser folder; the archive does not fabricate them. Some development code exists only inside the tables. The standalone scroll research tools correspond to abandoned experiments and are not recommended for use.

This history summarizes conversation evidence; it is not a claim that every internal edit is recoverable or that every feature was tested in every game mode.
